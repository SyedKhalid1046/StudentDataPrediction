Educational Intervention & Pedagogical Decision Framework

Evidence-based insights derived from multi-model feature importance rankings

Pedagogical Framework
01

Classroom Attendance is the #1 Behavioral Lever

Model coefficients and tree splits reveal that attendance rate below 80% dramatically increases failure probability by 4.2x. Maintaining attendance above 90% yields an expected final grade increase of +6.8 to +10.2 points.

Intervention: Automated attendance notification alerts to academic advisors when attendance drops below 85%.
02

Structured Study Hours Nonlinear Threshold

Weekly study hours demonstrate strong marginal gains up to 12-14 hours/week, after which returns plateau. Students studying under 6 hours/week have the highest vulnerability to failing exams.

Intervention: Establish weekly guided study hall sessions targeting high-risk students to achieve minimum 8 hrs/week.
03

Early Remediation for Prior Failures

A single previous course failure introduces significant negative inertia (-9.0 latent capability weight). Students with 1+ failures benefit dramatically from early diagnostic assessments and peer tutoring.

Intervention: Mandatory enrollment in supplementary tutoring for any student with prior failures.
04

Socioeconomic & Institutional Support Buffering

Access to home internet and strong family/institutional support compensates for background disparities, boosting predicted final performance by +3.5 to +5.0 points.

Intervention: Provide institutional loaner laptops/Wi-Fi hotspots and host parent engagement workshops.
